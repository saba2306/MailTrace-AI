Under The Hood

How MailTrace AI Solves This

THREAT SCORING FORMULA
Risk = Sender Rules + Route Trust + Hidden Triggers

Our algorithm checks three things at once: Did the real domain send it? Does the routing hop lead to a real office or an attacker gateway? Are there high-pressure money triggers?

1

Reverse Hop Traversal: An email travels like a courier parcel. We read the stamps backward to find the very first computer that sent it.

2

Private Subnet Stripping: Bypasses internal Wi-Fi routers (like 192.168.x.x) so we only trace the real public internet gateway.

3

Instant File Hashing: Locks a SHA-256 digital fingerprint so evidence cannot be questioned in court.
